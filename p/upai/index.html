A guided tour of six releases

Every model release is a user-facing event

Between October 2022 and December 2025, Reddit communities wrote hundreds of thousands of posts about conversational AI systems. We treat each major model release as an observation point and measure how sentiment and discussion themes shift in the weeks before and after. Six releases tell six very different stories.

What providers can take away

Five recurring patterns across the six releases, phrased as practical lessons for planning future model updates.

Close the expectation gap

Announce only what users can access at launch, or say clearly when and for whom advertised capabilities arrive. The distance between demo and reality dominated GPT-4o's reception.

Expectation Gap +19.4 pp after GPT-4o

Preserve model continuity

Removing models that users built workflows — and relationships — around triggers the sharpest backlash. Transition periods matter.

Feature Removal Frustration +6.8 pp after GPT-5

Ship capacity with capability

A technically admired model that people cannot reliably access still produces a negative reception. Release planning is also infrastructure planning.

Negative share +16.0 pp after DeepSeek R1

Release with a concrete use case

The most favorable shift in our data came from a model that arrived bundled with a clear workflow: Claude 4 with coding and agentic automation.

Positive +5.3 pp, negative −10.5 pp after Claude 4

Monitor perceptions continuously

Discussion themes move fast and backlash can be partially reversed: GPT-5.1 de-escalated much of the GPT-5 revolt within weeks.

Negative −4.3 pp after GPT-5.1
